AMBERCUT / PRIVACY

A private workshop.

Effective October 12, 2026
Android package: com.ambercut.workshop

Local storage

Projects, dimensions, offcuts and motion preferences are stored in this app’s private storage. The app has no internet permission, accounts, advertising, analytics or cloud sync. It does not transmit your project data. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled.

Files you choose

Exports and backups are written only to locations you choose through Android’s file picker. Restore reads the file you select. Files outside the app remain until you delete them separately. Sharing or saving through another provider is subject to that provider’s practices.

Deletion

Delete projects individually. Used or removed offcuts remain as internal history so completed projects retain their source reference. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all local data and preferences. Exported copies are not automatically removed.

Support

When you email support, your email provider sends your address and message to us. We use these only to respond and retain them only as needed. Send privacy or deletion requests to SanasithSeligmann@gmail.com.

Children and changes

This tool does not knowingly collect children’s personal data. Policy changes will be reflected in the effective date. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information through support.
